the right moment

An autonomous futures bot that waits for one thing — an inversion Fair Value Gap — then executes the trade.

TradingView KAIROS Broker

An impulse leaves a gap of unfilled orders. When price returns and closes back through it, that fair value gap inverts — old support becomes resistance. KAIROS reads that inversion off TradingView and fires on TopstepX in milliseconds.

iFVG is only the default. Create your strategy in Pine Script — point it at KAIROS and the bot runs the entries, stops, sizing and exits around your edge, executed the same way, every time.
